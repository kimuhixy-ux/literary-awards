Purpose of this site

World Literary Prizes Overview is an independently operated learning/reference app that lets you search and browse winners, winning works, and citations across major world literary prizes — including the Nobel Prize in Literature, the Booker Prize, and Japan's Akutagawa Prize. Citations are shown only as brief editorial summaries; full official citation texts and prize-lecture transcripts are not reproduced. See each prize's official site for full details.
